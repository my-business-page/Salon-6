THE BOTANICAL APPROACH

Understand.
Then treat.

Botanical Hair Salon is a home-based, appointment-led service in Woodlands. Its public listings highlight treatment expertise, suitability advice and personalised hair care.

01 · CONSULT

Start with the condition

Before chemical or intensive services, discuss your current hair texture, condition and previous treatments. This makes the service more deliberate rather than one-size-fits-all.

02 · DIAGNOSE

Look at the history

Previous bleaching, colouring, perming and straightening can change what is suitable. Share your hair history and recent photos so the appointment can be planned properly.

03 · TREAT

Repair with purpose

Public descriptions specifically mention Mucota treatments, Botanical Hair Botox and Aveda Botanical Dry/Damaged Hair Therapy, alongside colouring and other hair services.

04 · MAINTAIN

Keep the result healthy

Good aftercare matters. Ask what home routine, treatment interval and product use are appropriate for your hair after the appointment.

A HOME-BASED EXPERIENCE

Public local coverage describes the salon as home-based and appointment-only, with owner Kavin Ng bringing more than 20 years of salon experience. Reviews also highlight friendly advice and treatment choices based on hair condition.
